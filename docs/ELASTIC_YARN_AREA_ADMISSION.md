The repaired CPU96 bag drop still fails its strict `minimumTriangleArea >
1e-8 m²` gate. The saved frame-620 material face [1433, 1444, 1445] has
area **5.82899354 × 10⁻⁹ m²**. This research prototype tests a preventive
response for one represented material face; it has not been integrated into
the full cloth solver, and the failing drop remains retained.

For a proposed linear path of three FP64 owners, the squared cross product is
an exact polynomial of degree at most four. The candidate counts distinct
roots, including a tangent that touches the threshold without changing sign,
and isolates the earliest threshold event to at most `2⁻⁴⁴` of normalized
time. It requires strict area above the unchanged gate on the incoming
represented prefix. At a supported event, it applies a reciprocal area-rate
impulse to free owners, checks the *actual rounded* kinetic change and linear
and angular balance, and recertifies the complete post-response affine path.
An exhausted 128-node shared proof budget, moving pin, unsupported future
crossing, or lost represented margin rejects the candidate.

The original isolated helper admitted a face exactly equal to the strict
gate: a unit edge with perpendicular height `2 × 10⁻⁸ m` returned
`accepted=true` at area `1e-8 m²`. The independent test actually exits 1 on
that frozen source. A separate corrected candidate changes both helper
comparisons to `<=`; its strict-gate test exits 0. The full corrected
qualification exits 0 with a byte-identical result to the historical run.
Independent controls verify 48 analytic earliest roots, a terminal root,
a double tangent, four roots of a quartic, 64 conservatively bounded 3D
clear paths, and exact represented energy and momentum on 36 new admitted
velocity cases. The original qualifier separately covers 215 named temporal
cases and 128 represented velocity cases. These are CPU reference checks,
not native full-scene results.

The selected frame-0-to-frame-620 owners form a **synthetic linear chord**.
The source did not retain their intermediate positions or velocities, so a
response on that chord cannot be called an actual saved cloth trajectory.
The prototype also lacks a common earliest event over incident faces and
constraints, ownership of the native integrator's represented path, and a
ledger for all affected yarn, bending, contact, and external work. Local
kinetic passivity does not establish complete energy closure, material
calibration, or repaired fruit landing. The CPU96 full drop and spatial
convergence remain open.
